Heading: Linguistic Deception (Industry & Media Jargon)

Anthropomorphism: Innate Human tendency to Attribute Human Traits to Non Human Entities e.g. Machines/Software

We routinely use anthropomorphic metaphors to describe mechanical processes. This linguistic shorthand obscures how these systems actually function.

Anthropomorphic Phrase -> What It Implies

"The AI hallucinated" -> The system experienced a sensory error or a momentary lapse in its "knowledge."
"The AI decided..." -> The system exercised agency, weighing moral or logical options.
"The model understands..." -> The system has a conceptual, semantic grasp of reality.
**"The AI remembers..."** -> The system possesses a persistent mental archive, a unified identity over time, and an active cognitive capacity to recall past interactions to maintain a continuous, evolving relationship.
