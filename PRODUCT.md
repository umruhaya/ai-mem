# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Colleagues watching a live technical presentation on a large LED screen in a bright room. They may use LLM products regularly without knowing how conversational continuity is implemented.

## Product Purpose

Build an accurate mental model of “AI memory” by separating the stateless language model from the stateful software around it. Success means the audience can explain that an LLM only responds to its current input, while an agent harness constructs the appearance of memory through history, compaction, and retrieval.

## Brand Personality

Candid, mechanical, and lightly mischievous. The voice challenges familiar language without scolding the audience, then rewards curiosity with concrete visual explanations.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon-on-black AI imagery, glassy SaaS dashboards, abstract brain/circuit illustrations, text-dense lecture slides, and diagrams whose decoration is more prominent than their logic.

## Design Principles

- Begin with the misconception, then rebuild the model from first principles.
- Show the mechanism before naming the abstraction.
- Keep one dominant idea per slide and let continuity carry complexity across slides.
- Use humor as a reset, not as a substitute for explanation.
- Make hidden prompt construction visible whenever “memory” is discussed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast light surfaces, back-of-room legible type, keyboard and touch navigation, visible focus states, semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
